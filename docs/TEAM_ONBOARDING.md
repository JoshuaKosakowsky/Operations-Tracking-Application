## Choose a useful task

1. Open a Ready task and read its plain-language outcome and completion checklist.
2. Claim a concrete deliverable and expected check-in on the issue. Agree who leads
   and helps; move it to In Progress when work actually begins. Assignment alone is not progress.
3. Keep progress, questions, decisions and evidence on the issue. State what is
   finished, what comes next and any help needed.
4. Move completed work to Review and ask for verification. Close it after the
   stated acceptance checks pass; partial code or a slide outline is partial work.

Ready means available to start; Blocked means a named prerequisite is missing;
Deferred means deliberately postponed. Workflow shows status, Work breakdown
shows parent/child tasks, and Milestone roadmap groups all delivery tasks.
Keep the issue status label and board status aligned. Choose owners together;
a label or invitation does not assign a person's work.

## If you are coding

Use your own local clone and environment on each machine and exchange changes
through GitHub. Keep credentials and generated files local. Follow the
[README setup](../README.md#install-and-run-locally) and
[contribution workflow](../CONTRIBUTING.md); coding agents also read
[AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) and the current [handoff](../handoff.md).

Use focused branches and pull requests with actual validation. The dedicated
[Hermes reviewer](HERMES_REVIEWER.md) can review and merge eligible PRs, while
an author implements fixes. Automated JoshKozy review belongs to Joshua; it is
not another human or teammate's contribution. The owner currently keeps formal
branch/review safeguards inactive; this does not authorize a contributor to
publish unrelated work, change permissions or bypass future controls.

## Report a problem or a completed test

Describe what you tried, what you expected, what happened, and how to reproduce
it. Include the app version/commit and operating system/browser if known. Use
fictional data in examples; remove credentials and private employee, customer,
calendar and location information from screenshots or logs. Label sample versus
live-service results and say what remains unchecked.

Record substantive work in the [contribution record](CONTRIBUTIONS.md), including
design, testing, review and presentation work. Credit the actual person and
activity rather than counting invitations or automated review as team members.
